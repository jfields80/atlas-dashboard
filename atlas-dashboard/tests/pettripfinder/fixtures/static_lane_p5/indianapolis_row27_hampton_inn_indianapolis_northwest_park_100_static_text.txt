Among businesses, close to nature
Find us across from Park 100 businesses, just off I-465. Catch a show at the Pikes Performing Arts Center, a mile from our hotel. We're two miles from Eagle Creek Park's golf course, hiking trails, and wildlife centers. It's 25 minutes to both downtown Indianapolis' business district and the airport. Relax in our indoor pool. Hot breakfast and WiFi are free.
