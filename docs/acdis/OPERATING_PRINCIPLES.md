# Operating Principles

1. ACDIS remains inside the approved worktree at C:\Atlas-Grok.
2. ACDIS never modifies C:\Atlas or main.
3. Evidence categories stay distinct: Fact, Inference, Hypothesis, and Unknown.
4. Placeholder contracts are intentionally minimal and side-effect free.
5. Future workflow steps may involve manual research artifacts, but they are deferred beyond Phase 0.
