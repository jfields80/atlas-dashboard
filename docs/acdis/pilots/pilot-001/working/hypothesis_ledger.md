# Pilot-001 Hypothesis Ledger

Status key:
- UNTESTED: not yet validated
- SUPPORTED: materially supported by validation evidence
- REJECTED: materially contradicted by validation evidence
- INCONCLUSIVE: tested but evidence is insufficient to decide

## H1
Hypothesis:
Columbus consumers with a defined repair need value localized repair-cost guidance.

Status:
UNTESTED

Supporting evidence:
- FACT - official-site representation: RepairPal and Openbay present repair-cost guidance/estimate positioning on observed official pages.
- FACT - official-site representation: Oxford and other observed pages include Columbus-specific repair positioning and action prompts.

Contrary evidence:
- No direct consumer-demand outcome was measured in this pilot evidence pass.

Validation method:
- Gate 2 manual demand test with a single repair scope and tracked qualified submissions.

Success condition:
- A predefined minimum number of qualified submissions request localized repair-cost guidance within test duration.

Failure condition:
- Qualified submission volume remains below threshold or consumers do not engage with comparison delivery.

Evidence required to change status:
- Timestamped demand-test logs showing qualified submissions, completion, and follow-up responses.

Dependencies:
- Gate 1 supply pass
- One approved traffic source
- Operator-approved budget cap

## H2
Hypothesis:
Consumers value receiving at least two standardized local-shop responses for the same repair scope.

Status:
UNTESTED

Supporting evidence:
- FACT - official-site representation: Openbay represents quote/estimate actions and shop-list interactions.
- FACT - official-site representation: Multiple models expose discovery plus contact/booking actions, suggesting comparison intent is plausible.

Contrary evidence:
- No captured end-to-end evidence yet shows consumer preference for two-plus standardized responses.

Validation method:
- Gate 2 manual fulfillment that returns at least two standardized responses when available.

Success condition:
- Consumers indicate the comparison output is useful and would use it again or proceed with a participating shop.

Failure condition:
- Consumers show low engagement with comparison outputs or single-response dominance prevents comparability.

Evidence required to change status:
- Consumer follow-up responses and delivery logs linking intake to comparison usage outcomes.

Dependencies:
- Gate 1 comparable supply quality
- Standard quote template

## H3
Hypothesis:
At least three Columbus-area repair shops will participate in a manually fulfilled quote comparison without software integration.

Status:
UNTESTED

Supporting evidence:
- FACT - official-site representation: Oxford and other observed models expose direct contact and service action surfaces.
- INFERENCE: Manual outreach may be feasible because contact pathways are visible across observed local-provider surfaces.

Contrary evidence:
- No participation commitments have been recorded yet.

Validation method:
- Gate 1 outreach to Columbus-area shops with manual response template.

Success condition:
- At least three shops agree to provide manual responses in the standard format during the test window.

Failure condition:
- Fewer than three shops participate within outreach and time caps.

Evidence required to change status:
- Outreach log with contact attempts, responses, and participation outcomes.

Dependencies:
- Approved outreach script
- Operator time for manual follow-up

## H4
Hypothesis:
Participating shops can return sufficiently comparable responses using a standard quote template.

Status:
UNTESTED

Supporting evidence:
- FACT - official-site representation: Observed models expose estimate/booking/contact actions and service context.

Contrary evidence:
- No standardized multi-shop response set has been collected yet.

Validation method:
- Gate 1 template trial with participating shops and response-normalization review.

Success condition:
- Responses include required fields with enough consistency for side-by-side comparison.

Failure condition:
- Responses omit key fields or vary too widely for fair comparison.

Evidence required to change status:
- Collected template responses with field-completeness scoring and normalization notes.

Dependencies:
- Shop participation
- Stable template definition

## H5
Hypothesis:
A manually fulfilled comparison can be returned quickly enough to remain useful to the consumer.

Status:
UNTESTED

Supporting evidence:
- FACT - official-site representation: Existing products emphasize immediate actions (estimate/booking/contact), implying time sensitivity in user intent.

Contrary evidence:
- No measured manual turnaround times exist yet.

Validation method:
- Gate 2 track intake-to-delivery time for each qualified request.

Success condition:
- Median delivery time meets the operator-defined usefulness threshold.

Failure condition:
- Delivery times exceed threshold or consumers report comparison arrived too late.

Evidence required to change status:
- Timestamped intake and delivery logs plus follow-up usefulness responses.

Dependencies:
- Gate 1 supply responsiveness
- Operator capacity limits

## H6
Hypothesis:
Either consumers or participating repair shops will demonstrate a credible future willingness to support monetization.

Status:
UNTESTED

Supporting evidence:
- HYPOTHESIS from case file: likely payers may include local shops, specialty providers, or lead buyers.

Contrary evidence:
- No measured willingness-to-pay signal has been collected yet.

Validation method:
- Gate 2 follow-up questions to consumers and Gate 1 follow-up to shops on acceptable pricing models.

Success condition:
- Clear positive willingness signal from at least one payer side under a defined model.

Failure condition:
- No credible willingness signal from either side.

Evidence required to change status:
- Recorded pricing-preference responses, objections, and accepted model patterns.

Dependencies:
- Validated demand signal
- Validated supply signal
- Defined value proposition for each payer side
